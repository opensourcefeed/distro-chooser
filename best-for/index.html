Best Linux distros by need

The best Linux distribution depends on what you want to do with it. Each guide below explains what matters for one need, ranks a short list of distros using the same scoring as the Distro Chooser quiz, and says plainly who each pick is and isn't for.

  • Best Linux distros for beginnersFor most people trying Linux for the first time, Linux Mint is the safest choice. Ubuntu, Zorin OS and Pop!_OS are close behind, and the right one depends on your hardware and the desktop you prefer.
  • Best Linux distros for gamingIf you want a general-purpose desktop that also games well, Pop!_OS or Ubuntu are the safe picks. If gaming is the main job, a gaming-focused distro such as Bazzite, Nobara or CachyOS saves you setup time.
  • Best Linux distros for programmingFedora is the best all-round developer desktop, with recent toolchains and container tools built in. Ubuntu is the most compatible choice, and NixOS is the strongest if you want a fully reproducible setup.
  • Best Linux distros for privacy and securityThere is no single best privacy distro. Qubes OS gives the strongest isolation, Tails is built for anonymity, and Debian or Fedora make a private, secure everyday desktop for most people.
  • Best Linux distros for Windows usersLinux Mint is the easiest move from Windows for most people. Zorin OS is the closest look-alike, and Kubuntu suits people who like to customise.
